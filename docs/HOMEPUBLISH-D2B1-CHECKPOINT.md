### Internal ABI and finite limits

Anonymous SOCK_SEQPACKET channels created before fork; no filesystem socket or browser renew endpoint. hp_frame is local same-build native ABI (48 bytes on tested x86_64), uint64 generation/epoch/sequence, 16-byte getrandom token, uint32 operation/reserved. Operation 1 renew, 2 irrevocable stop; reserved must be zero, exact next sequence beginning 1, exact nonzero generation/epoch and token. Short/truncated/oversized/unknown/auth/stale/replayed frames fail closed. Drain at most 32 packets; flooding fails closed. STOP observed during a drain prevents pending renew from being applied; after close no event can resurrect the instance. These are internal-channel authorization checks, **not health validation**: a future controller must independently validate desired/no-stop/scope/revision/TLS/DNS/resources before each send. Same-UID trusted caller/inherited-fd ownership is assumed; this is not a security boundary against a compromised privileged controller or ptrace-capable peer.

Window 600ms, guardian poll 25ms, watchdog heartbeat timeout 200ms/poll 20ms, bounded reap 250ms. Continuous renew extends to receipt CLOCK_MONOTONIC +600ms while old authority is still valid; no restart, tested eight renewals over more than two guard windows with same live child PID and uninterrupted received ticks. Temporary duration 1..86400000ms (0 continuous) sets absolute initial monotonic limit before fork/provisioning; renew never moves it. Fixture 800ms temporary with five renewals still closes by original cutoff. These are internal test-stage budgets, **not product publication duration defaults or validated router worst-case timings**.

Watchdog is independent of controller and guardian process groups. It owns the data-child group, holds the unreaped leader identity (WNOWAIT), kills only that group/leader, uses guardian pidfd to terminate even stopped guardian, and bounded WNOHANG reaps. Data child has PDEATHSIG SIGKILL with parent-race check. Guardian socket EOF or absent heartbeat lets watchdog close ingress even after guardian SIGKILL/SIGSTOP; controller death/channel close or absent renew closes within finite window. Watchdog failure KILL is covered architecturally by child PDEATHSIG; watchdog SIGSTOP/entire scheduler stop is NOT independently tested or claimed fail-closed in this slice. Future hostile descendants/session escape, watchdog reciprocal STOP, inherited-fd sanitization/seccomp, production lifecycle/owner journal and secret-copy cleanup remain integration/security review gates. Never infer arbitrary process-tree guarantees from the trusted no-descendant loopback callback.
